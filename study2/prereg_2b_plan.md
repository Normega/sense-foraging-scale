## The validity battery for one score

What the 8 measures, from its content: noticing the senses with interest (N4), being able to move into the sensing mode (sf6_36, sfc_x2), treating it as worth the time (sf6_27), and whether pausing to take things in changes how stress lands (U4, reverse-keyed; it replaced sf6_44 on 2 November, decision 14). In 2a it correlated .80 with the trial's 10, so it is close to the Access + Valuing blend, not a new construct.

**What 2a already gives for the 8** (in-sample, same session, the 8 with U4): practice d = .67; vignette self-rating r = .33; IUS-12 r = −.18. Study 1 had no validity measures.

### Discriminant (in the current battery)

The strongest discriminant tests are **contrasts within one measure**, because they share method and differ only in content. Each is a dependent-correlation test (bootstrap difference, as the draft's H3b):

| Contrast | Prediction | What it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| BFI-2 Aesthetic Sensitivity > Intellectual Curiosity | yes | General openness or curiosity about ideas |
| FFMQ Observing > FFMQ Describing | yes | Mindfulness in general, verbal labelling |
| SBI Savoring the Moment > BFI-2 Open-Mindedness total | yes (the draft's H3b) | General appetite for experience |
| MAIA-2 Noticing > MAIA-2 Not-Worrying | yes | Low distress about body sensations |
| FFMQ Observing > SAMD Meaning-Detecting | yes (decision 16) | A spiritual or meaning-seeking openness: reading meaning into events rather than taking in what is there |

And bounds:
- BIDR-16 SDE and IM: 95% CI within ±.30 (the draft's H3c).
- PHQ-4, PSS-4: near zero to small negative. Reported, not tested; a large negative correlation would mean the scale is partly a wellbeing measure.

**A gap: no criterion outside self-report.** Every criterion above is something the participant reports. Options:
1. **A free-choice pause in the session (decided, decision 10).** For example: "If you like, take up to a minute to look and listen around you before the next page" (with Continue available at once). The criterion is whether they take it and for how long. No method overlap with the scale, and cheap. It needs a platform step and goes in the ethics amendment. **[Norm]**
2. **The PSY240 trial**, as sensitivity to change across the course. It is a separate dataset and only possible if the trial's 10 are linked to the 8 (see "To decide" in `decisions_after_2a.md`). A cross-study claim, not part of 2b.
3. Course game data (Delve, Tune) as behavioural foraging. Not in 2b's sample; for later.

## Hypotheses, single-score version (draft numbering)

- **H1 structure:** one-factor CFA + agreement factor on the 8 fits. Fit rule open (see `decisions_after_2a.md`).
- **H2 convergent:** FFMQ Observing, SBI Savoring, BFI-2 Aesthetic Sensitivity, MAIA-2 Noticing; CI lower bound > .30.
- **H3 discriminant:** (a) convergent upper bounds < .80; (b) the four within-measure contrasts, and FFMQ Observing > SAMD Meaning-Detecting; (c) BIDR within ±.30; (d) multiple R with the neighbour set < .80; (e) new measures' bounds, once chosen.
- **H4 criterion:** `crit_pause_days` positive; plus whichever of outdoors / following week / free-choice pause is promoted.
- **H5 incremental:** as the draft, one score.
- **H6 known groups:** d ≥ .50.
- ~~H7 stability~~: dropped with the follow-up (decision 12).
- **H8 understanding (new):** invariance across tells-apart groups. Direction: 2a predicts loadings will differ, so H8 is a test of whether they do, with the consequence stated (the score means something different for people who don't tell the modes apart).

Everything is tested on the 8; the 10 is reported beside it with the same rules.
