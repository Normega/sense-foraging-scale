# Study 2 (planned)

Confirmatory factor analysis of the Study 1 short form in a new Prolific sample, with validity measures:
FFMQ-15, MAIA-2, life satisfaction, PHQ-4 and BIDR-16 (8 of 16 items reverse-keyed, an external check on acquiescence).

Proposed from Study 1's results:

- Add a brief **openness to experience or savouring** measure, to test whether "Valuing and savouring" is distinct
  from a general appetite for experience.
- Model the four Study 1 factors plus a random-intercept agreement factor; test invariance across postings and countries.
- Recruitment by country to be set explicitly on Prolific (Study 1 ran internationally; see `../study1/README.md`).

Study 2 needs its own preregistration and amended materials before data collection.
